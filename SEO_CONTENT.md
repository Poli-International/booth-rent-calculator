#### H2: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

##### H3: What does the booth rent vs commission calculator compare?
The calculator compares your net income under two common tattoo studio business models: paying a fixed weekly booth rent plus supplies, versus paying a percentage of your revenue as commission to the studio.

##### H3: How is the break-even revenue calculated?
The break-even point is calculated using the formula: (weekly booth rent + booth supplies - commission supplies) / (commission percentage / 100). This shows the weekly revenue where both models produce equal income.

##### H3: What if my booth rent exceeds my weekly revenue?
The calculator will display a warning that booth rent exceeds weekly revenue, indicating you would be operating at a loss under the booth rent model at your current revenue level.

##### H3: Can I compare different commission percentages?
Yes, you can adjust the studio commission percentage from 0% to 100% to see how different commission rates affect your take-home pay compared to the booth rent model.

##### H3: What does the verdict banner tell me?
The verdict banner shows which model earns you more money per year at your current revenue, including the exact annual difference between the two models.

##### H3: Why do I need to enter supplies costs separately?
Supplies costs differ between models. Under booth rent, artists typically buy their own supplies. Under commission, the studio may provide supplies, or the artist may still need to purchase them separately.

##### H3: What happens if I enter zero weekly revenue?
The calculator will display an error message asking you to enter your revenue to compare models. Zero revenue cannot produce meaningful comparisons.

##### H3: How accurate are the annual projections?
Annual projections assume consistent weekly revenue across all working weeks. Actual income may vary due to seasonal fluctuations, holidays, and client demand.

##### H3: Can this calculator help me negotiate with a studio?
Yes, by showing the exact financial difference between models at your revenue level, you can use this data to negotiate better terms or choose the most profitable arrangement.

##### H3: What if both models show similar annual income?
The calculator will indicate both models produce similar income when the annual difference is less than £50, helping you focus on other factors like flexibility and studio amenities.
